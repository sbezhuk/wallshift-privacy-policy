🛡️ Summary: No Data Collection

WallShift does not collect, store, track, sell, or share any personal information or device data from its players. The game is rated 4+ and is completely safe and private for players of all ages, including children.

ℹ️ 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game WallShift ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Stepan Bezhuk for iOS devices.

We believe in complete privacy. WallShift is designed to provide fun and engaging gameplay without compromising your privacy or asking for any of your personal data.

🚫 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any information about you. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, ages, or any identifying details.
  • No Account Registration: There are no user accounts, passwords, or login requirements.
  • No Tracking & No Analytics: We do not track your activity, and we do not use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Facebook SDK).
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect IDFA (Advertising Identifier), IDFV, MAC address, or hardware identifiers.
  • No Location Data: We do not access or collect GPS, coarse location, or IP-based geolocation data.
  • No Device Permissions: The game does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or calendar.

💾 3. Local Data Storage on Your Device

All game progress and settings are stored strictly locally on your device using standard Apple iOS local storage (UserDefaults).

This includes:

  • High scores and game records
  • In-game virtual coins earned through gameplay
  • Unlocked cosmetic character themes and current theme selection
  • In-game achievements and personal milestones
  • Sound and music volume preferences
Important: This gameplay data never leaves your device. It is not transmitted over the Internet, and the developer has no access to it. You can erase all saved data at any time simply by deleting the app from your device.

👶 4. Children’s Privacy (Rated Age 4+)

WallShift has an age rating of 4+ (suitable for everyone aged 4 and older) and is designed to be family-friendly and child-safe.

  • Because our application does not collect any personal data whatsoever, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K), and Apple's Family & Kids Category guidelines.
  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal data from children under any age. Parents and guardians can be confident that their children's privacy is 100% protected while playing WallShift.

🔗 5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

WallShift does not use third-party tracking services or data brokers. No personal data or user behavior is shared with or sold to any third party.

⚖️ 6. Your Rights

Under global privacy laws (including GDPR and CCPA), you have the right to privacy and data control. Because we do not collect or store any of your personal data on any server, there is no personal data to view, export, or delete on our end. All local game progress remains solely in your control on your iOS device.

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be published here with an updated "Last updated" date.

Усі налаштування та досягнення зберігаються виключно локально на вашому пристрої за допомогою стандартного системного сховища Apple iOS (UserDefaults).

До цих даних належать:
